Climate Change and Renewable Energy

Climate change represents one of the greatest challenges facing humanity today. Rising global temperatures, extreme weather events, and rising sea levels threaten ecosystems and communities worldwide.

Renewable energy sources offer a path forward:
- Solar power has become increasingly cost-effective
- Wind energy provides clean electricity at scale
- Hydroelectric power offers reliable baseload generation
- Geothermal energy taps into Earth's natural heat
- Battery storage enables grid stability

Transitioning to renewable energy is essential for reducing greenhouse gas emissions and building a sustainable future for generations to come.
